      Sets

      One idler wheel and one drive wheel in a single set. In sets no. 1–3 the idler wheel is the same: two 6208 2RS C3 bearings, a W80 ring, a 6PK1500 poly-V belt, balanced and ready for a Ø40 axle. Only the drive wheel changes. Sets no. 4 and 5 are Ø600 wheels: both wheels have three bearings each, and the set price is the sum of the two wheels. If you only need one wheel, its price is further down, in the single wheels price list.

Each has an idler wheel and a combined drive wheel with a Ø330 pulley for two B-17 belts. Both wheels have three 6208 2RS C3 bearings and a W80 ring. The pair is set no. 4 or set no. 5.

      Ø700 wheels

      700 mm diameter wheels with a 9PK poly-V belt. Grey cast iron, ground bearing seats. Double angular contact bearings. The drive wheel has a Ø415 pulley for two B-17 V-belts — combustion engine drive possible. The combined wheel offset allows the use of band saw blades up to 60 mm wide. These two wheels together are set no. 6.
